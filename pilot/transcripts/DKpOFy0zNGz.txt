I met a guy online. It started with casual chats, movies, music, life's little oddities. He had this way of making mundane topics feel alive. After weeks of talking, he asked, why don't we meet? The real coffee is better than virtual chats. I agreed, nervous but curious. When we finally met, it was at a quite cafe. He looked just like his photos. But there was something more, an energy, a warmth. Over coffee, he shared his struggles, the things he didn't talk about online. He had lost a lot, family, trust, himself. I'm trying to piece things together, he said, his voice breaking. That day I decided to stay in his life. Over the months I became his late night call, his safe space, he cried, he grieved and he healed. I was there for every moment. But when he finally rebuilt himself, he drifted away. I'm ready to move forward, he said and smiled through the hurt. Now I sit with the memories and realize sometimes we are meant to be chapters in someone else's story, even if they are a whole book. Not all connections last forever, but they teach us the value of love, support and letting go.
